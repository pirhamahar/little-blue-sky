WHAT IT TOOK TO GET HERE

Some Things Only We Understand ❤️

I don't think anyone else could ever truly understand what this distance has been like for us.

The missed moments, the days when we just wanted to be together, and all the little things that suddenly became difficult because we couldn't simply be there for each other.

But somehow, through all of it, we kept choosing each other.

And I think that's what makes us so special. ❤️
