OUR LITTLE STORY

And Somehow, It Became Us ❤️

It all started at university... You were my senior, and somehow you always seemed to be around my class. 👀

Then you got my number through a friend. But instead of messaging me, you just saved it. Very smart move, because Instagram did the rest. 😂

You sent me a request, I took my time, and eventually we started talking.

You said you messaged because you were my senior and would help me if I ever needed it. And look where that explanation brought us. ❤️

From strangers, to friends, to something I never want to lose. ❤️
